Privacy Policy

Lights On, Robots Off (Android game), developed by DobroByte Studio. Effective date: 18 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the game Lights On, Robots Off (the "Game") processes, why, who may receive it, how long it is kept, and how you can control or delete it. DobroByte Studio, an independent developer based in Ukraine ("we"), is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: dobrobyte.studio@gmail.com.

The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, camera or microphone access. It does not request Android location permissions and does not collect GPS location (see section 2 about approximate location inferred by advertising services).

1. Information processed by the Game

Local game data

Your progress and settings (heroes, equipment, currency, upgrades, game settings) are stored on your device. Depending on your Android version, device maker and Android backup settings, Android may back up this local data to your Google account and restore it on another device. This is a function of the Android system, controlled in your device settings; it is separate from the Game's own cloud backup below.

Cloud backup

While the Game has internet access, it saves your game progress to our backend hosted on Supabase (project region: EU, Ireland, eu-west-1). The server stores: your current cloud save; the previous version of the cloud save (for recovery); a pseudonymous profile identifier; and a cryptographic hash of your random recovery code (not the code itself). You keep the recovery code. Anyone who knows the code can restore the backup, so keep it private.

Leaderboard

The arena leaderboard stores your best arena result, your display name, your avatar and the pseudonymous profile linked to the result. Your display name, avatar and result are visible to other players. Do not use your real name, contact details or other personal information as a display name. See section 5 for rules and reports.

Technical server information

When the Game contacts our backend, Supabase and its infrastructure may process technical request data (IP address, time, request metadata, security logs) so that we can operate the service, keep it secure, investigate errors and prevent abuse.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded video ads (ads you choose to watch in exchange for a reward) through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The SDK and Google may collect and use, for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and security: your IP address; product interactions (such as taps and video views); diagnostic and performance information; the Android Advertising ID when available and permitted; and the App Set ID and other device or app identifiers. The Game does not request location permissions; Google may nevertheless infer approximate location from your IP address. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent

Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) the Game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings, Ad settings. On Android you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in the device privacy settings. Choosing non-personalised ads limits personalisation but ads may still use some identifiers for purposes such as frequency capping and aggregated reporting.

3. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Cloud backup, restore and leaderboard: necessary to provide the features you request, and our legitimate interest in operating them.
  • Security and technical logs: our legitimate interests in security, reliability and abuse prevention.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad-related storage: your consent where consent is required by law.
  • Support and privacy requests: responding to your request, exercising or defending legal claims, and legal compliance.

4. Who receives data

Supabase hosts the backend (cloud saves, leaderboard, technical logs). Data is stored in the EU (Ireland); Supabase may use subprocessors and, where required, relies on contractual safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Google (AdMob) and participating advertising providers receive the advertising data described in section 2 and may process it in other countries under Google's safeguards. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Leaderboard names, reports and moderation

Display names are visible to other players. Names that are offensive, hateful, sexual, impersonate others or contain personal or contact information are not allowed. You can report a name from the leaderboard screen; we review reports and may change or hide a name and remove the related leaderboard entry. Players who repeatedly break these rules may be removed from the leaderboard. Contact us at dobrobyte.studio@gmail.com to report a name outside the Game.

6. Retention

  • Cloud saves (current and previous version) and the pseudonymous profile are kept while the cloud backup exists or until you delete it.
  • Leaderboard records are kept while the cloud profile exists, unless removed earlier.
  • Server and security logs are kept for the period set by our hosting provider and only as long as reasonably necessary.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google and its partners under their own policies.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer and document the request, or as required by law.

7. Deleting your data

In the Game open Settings, Cloud backup, Delete copy: this deletes your current and previous cloud saves, the cloud profile and the linked leaderboard records. You can also ask for deletion at dobrobyte.studio@gmail.com; if you cannot use the in-game function, we may need your recovery code to identify the cloud profile. If you email a recovery code, your email provider processes that message; we use the code only to process the request. Copies in routine infrastructure logs or provider backups may remain for a limited time until they expire. The Game has no accounts, so there is no separate account to close. Local data is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data, but data restored or kept by Android's own backup is controlled in your device and Google account settings.

8. Security and international transfers

The Game connects to our backend over encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We are based in Ukraine and use providers such as Supabase and Google, so information may be processed in countries other than yours; where the law requires, safeguards such as contractual clauses are used.

9. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (for example the GDPR) you may have the right to information, access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawal of consent at any time (withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing). Because the Game has no accounts, we may be unable to link data to you without your recovery code. You can use the in-game controls or contact us. If the GDPR applies you may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in the country where you live or work. Users in Ukraine may contact the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

10. Children

The Game is not designed or marketed primarily to children, and we do not ask for a child's real name, email, phone, precise location, photos, contacts, camera or microphone data. If you believe personal data of a child was processed without a required parental consent, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy if the Game, our providers, the law or our practices change. The effective date above shows the latest revision. Where a change materially affects how personal data is processed, we will give additional notice where required, for example in the Game or on its store page.
