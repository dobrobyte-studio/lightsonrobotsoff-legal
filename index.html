Privacy Policy

Lights On, Robots Off (Android game) by DobroByte Studio. Effective date: 18 October 2026.

This policy explains what information the game Lights On, Robots Off (the "Game") collects, why, who receives it, and how you can control or delete it. The Game does not require an account, does not ask for your real name, email or phone number, and does not use your location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Your game save is also stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal data.

2. Advertising and consent

The Game shows rewarded video ads (ads you choose to watch in exchange for a reward) through Google AdMob. Google may use your advertising ID and other data as described in Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings, Ad settings. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the device settings.

3. Cloud backup and leaderboard

The cloud backup is created automatically while the Game has internet access. The recovery code is random and is the only key to your backup: keep it private. Anyone who knows the code can restore the backup. The leaderboard shows only your display name, avatar and results.

4. Retention and deletion

Cloud data is kept while the backup exists. You can delete your cloud backup (and the linked leaderboard entries) in the Game: Settings, Cloud backup, Delete. You can also request deletion by email at the address below, giving your recovery code. Local data is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets that age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. International transfers and security

Data may be processed outside your country by our server provider and by Google. Requests to our server use encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Use the in-game controls above or contact us.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will also be announced in the Game or on its store page.
